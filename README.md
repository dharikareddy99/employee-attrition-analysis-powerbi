# Employee Attrition Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview
An interactive Power BI project analysing employee attrition to identify workforce patterns, understand employee segments with higher turnover, and support data-driven retention decisions.

## Business Objective
The objective is to investigate employee attrition patterns, identify groups with higher attrition rates, and highlight areas where further investigation may help improve employee retention.

## Tools & Technologies
- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Data Modelling
- Data Visualisation

## Dataset Summary

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Employees | 4,410 |
| Active Employees | 3,699 |
| Employees Who Left | 711 |
| Overall Attrition Rate | 16.12% |

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Workforce Overview
- Overall attrition rate and employee headcount
- Active versus departed employees
- Key workforce metrics and attrition patterns

### 2. Who Is Leaving?
- Attrition by age band and tenure
- Attrition by marital status and gender
- Years with current manager
- Job-role analysis
- Age and tenure heatmap
- High-risk employee segment analysis

### 3. Factors Associated with Attrition
- Work-life balance
- Job involvement and satisfaction
- Business travel frequency
- Monthly income bands
- Salary hike bands

## Key Insights
- Overall attrition in the dataset is 16.12%.
- First-year employees have an attrition rate of approximately 34.9%.
- Employees aged 25 or younger have an attrition rate of approximately 35.8%.
- Employees reporting bad work-life balance have an attrition rate of approximately 31.4%.
- Employees who travel frequently have an attrition rate of approximately 24.9%.
- Attrition rates vary across departments, employee age groups, and tenure bands.

## Technical Implementation
The report uses interactive Power BI visuals, KPI cards, matrix heatmaps, slicers, and DAX measures to explore attrition patterns.

Key metrics include:
- Total employee headcount
- Employees who left
- Attrition rate
- Share of leavers
- High-risk group attrition rate
- High-risk group share of active employees

## Business Recommendations
1. Investigate onboarding and role clarity for employees in their first year.
2. Explore workload and work-life balance concerns.
3. Examine whether frequent business travel contributes to employee dissatisfaction.
4. Investigate manager support and employee experience across tenure groups.
5. Monitor attrition over time to evaluate retention initiatives.

## Limitations
- The analysis is descriptive and does not establish causation.
- Missing survey responses may affect some findings.
- Attrition percentages for small employee groups should be interpreted cautiously.
- The high-risk segment is an analytical grouping, not a predictive model.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates the use of Power BI and DAX to analyse workforce data, identify employee attrition patterns, and communicate actionable insights through interactive dashboards.

The analysis helps prioritise areas for further investigation and supports evidence-based workforce decisions.
